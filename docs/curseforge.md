## Description

A cozy-adjacent kitchen-sink modpack for Minecraft 1.21.1 (NeoForge): world, QoL, tech and building mods, tuned to run well. No magic.

Explore pretty biomes with friends, build a home worth coming back to, and duck into the mining dimension when you need ore. There's enough tech and automation to make a base hum, a guided quest book, and room for the people who just want to decorate. It's built to run on an average laptop (including a MacBook) or a Steam Deck, with shaders, under 6 GB of RAM.

### A quest book written for this pack

A guided book from your first night to the endgame. Every chapter is open from day one and nothing in the pack is locked behind a quest, so follow it or wander off. Chapters split into lines you can follow side by side, and the tech chapters cover their mods in detail, down to every seed, generator and pipe.

Rewards come in crates, from humble Kindling to grand Ember, and a repeatable Bounty Board keeps your farms and factories busy. The Allthemodium Alloy Paxel takes one finished piece from every tech line and forges it into a single tool. At the very end, enough power to break the universe makes the Spark of Creation, a catalyst for creative items.

### What's in it

- **Exploring:** Terralith biomes, Incendium's Nether, Nullscape's End, Moog's sky islands, YUNG's structures, Towns and Towers, Formations, Structory, Explorify, paragliders with heart containers and stamina, waystones and JourneyMap
- **Mining:** the Allthemodium mining, Other and Beyond dimensions, Mining Gadgets, Allthemodium gear, one set of ores from AllTheOres
- **Building:** Macaw's, Handcrafted, FramedBlocks, Supplementaries, Cluttered, Twigs, Building Gadgets
- **Tech:** Mekanism, Refined Storage, Powah, Flux Networks, Oritech, Mystical Agriculture with Botany Pots, Pylons and Modular Routers
- **Cozy:** Farmer's Delight and Croptopia food, Comforts, Hourglass, Cooking for Blockheads, ambient sounds, auroras, falling leaves, fireflies and other small particle details
- **Shaders:** Complementary Reimagined and Unbound (with Euphoria Patches), BSL and MakeUp Ultra Fast, included and off by default
- **Controllers:** Controlify, for gamepads and the Steam Deck

### Requirements

- At least 4 GB of RAM allocated, 6 GB recommended (8 GB with a heavy shader)

### Playing with friends

A ready-to-run server pack is attached to every release under **Additional Files**. The [server guide](https://github.com/cosmicspork/hearth-and-hollow/blob/main/docs/server.md) covers setup, settings, backups and pregenerating the world.

### Found a problem?

If the game crashes, the Crash Assistant window can upload your logs and open an issue for you. You can also [open an issue on GitHub](https://github.com/cosmicspork/hearth-and-hollow/issues/new) directly. Please say what you were doing when it happened.
